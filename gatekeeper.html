Your API needs a bouncer, not a bottleneck.

Gatekeeper hands out a fixed number of tickets and refills them at a steady pace. Spend them fast if you like. When they run out, the request gets a 429 stamped on it.

Every call to your API reaches the gate before it reaches your code. Nothing gets served yet.

then

It hands over a ticket

If there's a ticket in the bin, the request walks through and the bin loses one. Bursts are fine while the bin lasts.

finally

No tickets, no entry

An empty bin means an instant 429. Tickets drip back in at a steady rate, so traffic recovers without anyone touching a thing.
